What's inside

The one-day plan: an hour-by-hour schedule of about 8.5 hours of study, with breaks. It gives Domain 3 the most time because it is 28% of the exam. Start times are flexible.
All five domains, each explained as what it is, why it matters and how the exam tests it, and how it works. Each domain ends with common traps and a "cover the page and answer aloud" recall drill.
Look-alike services: a page of pairs the exam mixes up, such as Rekognition vs Textract and RAG vs fine-tuning vs continued pre-training.
Service picker and keyword decoder: scenario-to-service tables, plus what phrases like "least operational overhead" or "without retraining" usually point to.
One-page cheat sheet to read just before the exam.
30 practice questions with an answer key that explains why each answer is right, and a score guide.
Glossary and exam-day playbook: about 40 flashcard terms, and how to read and pace questions.
